Proposed direction

Product
Mentorship through profiles, relevant discovery and consent-based introductions.
Character
Composed, welcoming, precise.
World
Aviation arrival and a professional alumni workspace. Preserve the logo and red accent.
Moment
The window flight at entry; actual shared skills and mentor context in the workspace.
Signature
Concentric corners, with sky material reserved for account doorways.

The doorway feels like arrival; the workspace makes the next relevant introduction clear.

What the audit found

  • Login and registration use conflicting corner, surface and control treatments.
  • Strong borders and heavy headings compete across shared components.
  • Marketing copy repeats the same promise in several places.
  • Generic empty states and separate password controls add avoidable chrome.
  • Discovery should give more emphasis to actual profile evidence and less to containers.
